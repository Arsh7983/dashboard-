Interactive Sales & Profit Dashboard | Microsoft Excel | Data Analysis & Visualization

Description:

                   
     An interactive Excel dashboard that tracks sales, profit, customer count, product categories, monthly trends, top  
     customers, and state-wise performance to support data-driven business decisions.

Tools & Technologies

                     Microsoft Excel: Data cleaning, filtering, sorting, and dashboard creation.
                     Power Query: Data transformation, cleaning, and preparation.
                     Power BI: Data visualization and business insights.

Data Source-Platform: 
                           
                     Kaggle, Sample Superstore Sales Dataset Includes Sales, profit, orders, customers, products,                               discounts, and geographical information.
Business Problem-

                    Businesses need a clear view of sales and profitability to identify performance trends, top customers,                     and profitable product categories.
Dashboard Goal:

                    To track sales, measure profitability, and uncover business insights.

Key Features & Highlights:
                   
                    KPI tracking: Total Sales, Total Profit, and Customer Count.
                          Category-wise sales and profit analysis.
                          Monthly sales and yearly profit trends.
                          Top 5 customers by profit.
                          State-wise sales distribution.
                           Interactive filters for exploring business performance.
Business Impact & Insights-
 
                           Identifies top-performing product categories and customers.
                           Highlights monthly sales and yearly profit trends.
                           Reveals regional sales performance.
                           Helps identify growth opportunities and improve business decisions
